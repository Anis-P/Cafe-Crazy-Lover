Our Story

Where every cup carries a little madness, and a lot of love.

Founded in 2019 by Chaitanya Sapte, Cafe Crazy Lover started as a small dream — to brew the kind of coffee that makes people stop, smile and stay a little longer. Seven years and thousands of happy regulars later, we're still pouring that same passion into every cup.

From slow-pulled espressos to chef-crafted pizzas, every item on our menu is made with locally sourced premium ingredients and a whole lot of obsession.
