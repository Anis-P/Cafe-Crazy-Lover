Meet Chaitanya Sapte

What started in 2019 as a one-room dream is today a beloved neighborhood ritual. Chaitanya wanted to build a cafe where the coffee was world-class, the food felt like home, and the people behind the counter knew your name.

Seven years on, that's exactly what we are. A little crazy, a lot loving, and obsessed with the details — from bean to brew, dough to plate.
